## 🧮 Resolution Rules (for AI)

```
Action Result =
Commander Mobility
+ Action Bonus
+ Average Mobility of Commanders
+ Dice (1d20)
```

### Action Bonuses
- Full speed ahead: +8 (morale −5)  
- Forced march: +12 (morale −15, heavy loss on failure)  
- Rest: +0 (morale +15)  
- Negotiation: +5 (success gives advantage next turn)  
- Reconnaissance: +3 (next turn check +5)
