# 909. 【Generative AI】Zhuge Liang Summons the Southeastern Wind at Red Cliffs to Proclaim the Tech Stack as “THE BEST”

topics: ["GenerativeAI", "PromptEngineering", "GitHub", "GitHubPages", "Mermaid", "Python", "VSCode"]

---

![Zhuge Liang summoning the southeastern wind and promoting the tech stack](../../assets/images/900/909_Zhuge-wind.png)

## 📌 Purpose of This Article

This article explores how far generative AI image output can be pushed  
when **a historical figure and a well-known symbolic episode** are explicitly specified.

While articles 907 and 908 focused on modern TV-advertisement styles,  
this entry shifts direction completely by combining:

- *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*  
- The Battle of Red Cliffs  
- Zhuge Liang (Kongming)  
- The legendary summoning of the southeastern wind  
- The dragon as a symbol of the “Sleeping Dragon”  

The goal is to generate a **mythic, high-impact promotional image**  
that proclaims a modern tech stack as “THE BEST.”

---

## 🎯 Key Points of This Attempt

- The following tech stack is explicitly included:
  - GitHub  
  - GitHub Pages  
  - Mermaid  
  - Python  
  - VSCode  
- The emphasis is not technical explanation, but **symbolism and visual impact**
- English and Traditional Chinese are intentionally mixed for global readability  

Generative AI tends to strongly favor  
**named characters, iconic scenes, and symbolic elements**  
such as wind, fire, and dragons.

---

## ⚡ Base Prompt Used (English + Traditional Chinese)

```text
A photorealistic cinematic scene inspired by the Battle of Red Cliffs.

Zhuge Liang stands on a cliff, holding a feather fan,
dramatically summoning the southeastern wind.
His expression is calm, confident, and legendary.

A massive dragon appears in the background,
symbolizing the Sleeping Dragon.

Stormy sky, powerful wind, burning ships below.
Epic, realistic lighting, ultra-detailed, movie still quality.

Top headline text:
“GitHub! GitHub Pages! Mermaid! Python! VSCode!”

Big central punch text in Traditional Chinese:
「諸葛亮借東南風」
「最棒！」

Sub text in English:
“Zhuge Liang summons the southeastern wind — THE BEST!”

No real brand logos, use icon-like visuals.
High resolution, sharp readable text, no watermark.
```

---

## 🧩 Prompt Elements That Worked Well

### 1️⃣ Explicit Historical Names and Events

```text
Zhuge Liang
Battle of Red Cliffs
southeastern wind
```

Using specific names produces far more stable results  
than abstract descriptions such as “ancient strategist.”

---

### 2️⃣ Adding a Symbolic Element (Dragon)

```text
A massive dragon appears in the background
symbolizing the Sleeping Dragon
```

This prevents the background from becoming visually flat  
and reinforces the legendary status of the character.

---

### 3️⃣ Steering Toward Photorealism

```text
photorealistic
cinematic
movie still quality
ultra-detailed
```

These terms help suppress anime-style outputs  
and push the image toward a realistic, cinematic look.

---

## ⚠ Common Failure Modes

| Issue | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too fantasy-oriented | Explicitly specify *photorealistic* |
| Anime-style faces | Add *cinematic* / *movie still* |
| Broken text | Reduce total text volume |
| Logo distortion | Avoid real logos, use icon-like visuals |

## ✅ Conclusion

- Generative AI responds extremely well to **history, symbolism, and iconic scenes**
- The same tech stack can be promoted in radically different ways  
  simply by changing the visual narrative
- Combining **technology with historical motifs** creates strong visual hooks  

Article 907 demonstrated a *generic* approach,  
908 pushed *cultural exaggeration*,  
and **909 represents a full commitment to historical symbolism**.

That concludes this example.
