## ❄️ Winter Campaign Phase

- Focus: **Encirclement, bombardment, defense**
- The castle does not fall, but **attrition accumulates**

### Winter Campaign Actions
1️⃣ Castle sortie 🔥  
2️⃣ Strengthen encirclement 🐢  
3️⃣ Bombardment / siege 💣  
4️⃣ Repair defenses 🛠  
5️⃣ Peace negotiations 🤝  

---

## 🌞 Summer Campaign Phase

- Starts with **attrition carried over** from winter  
- Focus: **Field battles and final decision**

### Summer Campaign Actions
1️⃣ Decisive frontal battle ⚔️  
2️⃣ Flanking assault 🌀  
3️⃣ Defensive formation 🛡  
4️⃣ All-out assault 🔥  

---

## 🧮 Resolution Rules (for AI)

```
Battle Result =
Commander Leadership
+ Action Bonus
+ Average Commander Stats
+ Dice (1d20)
− Accumulated Attrition Penalty
```

### Attrition Penalty
- Damage taken during the Winter Campaign carries over  
- Heavy losses apply penalties of −5 to −15

---

## ⚡ Highlight Events

### 🔥 The Battle of Sanada-maru
- Condition: Toyotomi sortie succeeds greatly  
- Effect: Tokugawa morale −30  

### 🐢 Complete Encirclement
- Condition: Tokugawa succeeds in encirclement repeatedly  
- Effect: Toyotomi morale −25  

---

## 🏁 Victory Conditions

### Toyotomi Side
- Force the Tokugawa commander to retreat in the Summer Campaign  
- Maintain morale at 50 or higher  

### Tokugawa Side
- Defeat the Toyotomi commander in the Summer Campaign  
- Reduce Toyotomi troops to 20% or less
