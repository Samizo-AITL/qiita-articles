## 🧮 Resolution Rules (for AI)

```
Battle Result =
Commander Leadership
+ Action Bonus
+ Average Combat of Commanders
+ Dice (1d20)
```

### Action Bonuses
- Frontal attack: +0  
- Flanking attack: +5 (extra damage on success)  
- Night raid: +8 (enemy morale −15 on success)  
- Defend HQ: +5 (advantage next turn)

---

## ⚡ Highlight Event: Duel (Hype Moment)

### Conditions
- Both armies’ morale ≥ 50  
- Player chooses “Duel”

### Check
```
Combat + Dice (1d20)
```

### Outcome
- Win: Enemy army morale −30 💥  
- Lose: Own army morale −30 😱  
- Draw: Both sides morale −10  

---

## 🏁 Victory Conditions

- Force the enemy commander to retreat  
- Reduce enemy troops to 30% or less  
- Reduce enemy morale to 0
