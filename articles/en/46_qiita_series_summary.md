# 46. 【Final】 Summary of the Qiita Series and Future References 🏁
topics: ["Qiita", "GitHub", "Zenn", "Semiconductor", "Control"]

---

This article concludes the Qiita series, which ran through Article 45.  
Article 46 serves as the final summary.

The articles published in this series are not intended to be complete on Qiita alone.  
They are positioned as **introductory and foundational materials** that guide readers toward more detailed and practical technical resources.

---

## 📘 Positioning of the Qiita Articles

The articles published on Qiita are intended as **foundational and overview-oriented content** for understanding the technical materials published on the official portal site:

[https://samizo-aitl.github.io/](https://samizo-aitl.github.io/)

On Qiita, the focus is on presenting the overall picture and prerequisite knowledge.  
More detailed and large-volume technical content is managed on other platforms.

---

## 🗂 Management of Article Sources and Supplementary Materials

All Markdown sources of the Qiita articles, as well as supplementary articles not published on Qiita, are centrally managed in the following GitHub repository:

- **qiita-articles**  
  [https://samizo-aitl.github.io/qiita-articles](https://samizo-aitl.github.io/qiita-articles)

This repository includes:

- 📝 Source files of all Qiita articles  
- 📚 Fundamental knowledge articles (No.001–)  
- 🧪 Free-form articles organized in the 900 series  
- 🌍 A complete English version prepared for international readers

---

### 📖 Fundamental Knowledge Articles (No.001–)

The fundamental knowledge articles are prepared to supplement the prerequisites required to read the Qiita articles.  
They include content that is not published on Qiita and are positioned as **supporting reference materials**.

---

### 🧩 Articles in the 900 Series

The 900-series articles are based on previously published Qiita articles and are intended for **free-form organization and expansion**, without being constrained by Qiita’s format.

Once these articles reach a sufficient level of completeness, publishing them again on Qiita will be considered.

---

## ✍ Zenn Articles

In addition to Qiita, technical articles are also published on Zenn.  
Zenn focuses on **more technically detailed or theme-specific content** compared to Qiita.

The source files for Zenn articles are managed in the following repository:

- **zenn-articles**  
  [https://samizo-aitl.github.io/zenn-articles](https://samizo-aitl.github.io/zenn-articles)

## 🏗 Official Portal (Main Site)

The official portal site:

[https://samizo-aitl.github.io/](https://samizo-aitl.github.io/)

hosts technical materials that exceed what can reasonably be covered on Qiita or Zenn, focusing primarily on:

- ⚙ Semiconductor-related topics (processes, devices, equipment, and physical background)
- 🤖 Practical control methodologies using AITL

Qiita and Zenn serve as **introductory and organizational entry points**, while detailed technical materials are continuously maintained on GitHub and the official portal.

This article concludes the Qiita series at Article 45 and serves as its final summary.
