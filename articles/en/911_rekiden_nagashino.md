## 🧮 Resolution Rules (for AI)

```
Battle Result =
Commander Leadership
+ Action Bonus
+ Average Commander Stats
+ Dice (1d20)
```

### Action Bonuses
- Cavalry charge: +8 (countered charges cause recoil damage)  
- Firearms attack: +8 (consecutive success lowers enemy morale)  
- Flanking maneuver: +5  
- Defensive formation: +5 (damage reduction)
