## Everything Went Wrong. It Still Worked.

topics: ["Generative AI"]

---

## Overview

This article presents a case study in which an image was generated by an image generation AI  
using a prompt composed **only of emojis**.

No textual instructions were provided.

## Notes

- The input consists only of emojis  
- Temporal flow and emotional transitions are interpreted by the AI

## Conclusion

Image generation can succeed even with emoji-only prompts.  
While controllability is reduced, a certain level of coherence can still be achieved.
